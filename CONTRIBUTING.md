# Local contributions

The maintainer of record is @Mister-Meeseeks (from 2026-10-21). No contribution is accepted as a scientific result until a qualified scientific reviewer has reviewed it. Store new experiments under research/<case>/ with exact input hashes, commands, measurements, uncertainties, negative controls, limitations and a pending review record. Keep current control snapshots immutable. A changed catalogue, orbit, timestamp, threshold or calibration method creates a new result version.

Contributors may receive an agreed contributors-file entry, acknowledgement or citation. Scientific co-authorship requires substantive contribution and agreement; agent execution alone does not imply authorship. The historical detections are credited to A. Gnädig and A. Doppler; G. V. Williams measured the later trail and remeasured the earlier one, as documented in MPEC 1999-N21. This pilot is an automated local replication, with no named human reviewer.
